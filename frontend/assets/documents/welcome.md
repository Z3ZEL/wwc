## Welcome to {{site_name}}

A map of campsites, shared by campers for campers.

- **Browse** the map and click a marker to see a campsite's photos, ratings and comments.
- **Search** by name, or use **Filters** to find the spots that suit you.
- **Locate me**, at the bottom-right, centers the map on where you are. Your position stays in your browser.
- **Sign up** to add your own campsites, rate and comment. Locations are public, so only share places that can be shared.

The **Updates** tab lists what changed in each new version.

By using the site you agree to the [Terms of Use](terms.md). How your data is handled: [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
